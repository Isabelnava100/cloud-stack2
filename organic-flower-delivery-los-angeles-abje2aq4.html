How long will my organic flowers last?

With proper care, our organic flowers typically last between 5 to 7 days, and sometimes longer. We include care instructions with every delivery to help you maximize the lifespan and enjoy the beauty of your arrangement. Factors like room temperature and humidity can affect longevity.
